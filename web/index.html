    Opening your camera…

    A page to a performance.

    1. Add a photo. Use the rear camera or upload a clear JPEG, PNG or WebP of printed staff notation. Include clefs, key signatures, and complete measures. Handwriting, tablature and very skewed photos are not reliable.
    2. Read and review. “Read the notes” uploads the photo only to the server hosting this app. Audiveris performs real optical recognition there. It can take up to three minutes per photo. Review the pitches, warnings and exported MusicXML; confidence percentages are not available.
    3. Choose your sound. Select piano, violin or cello, acknowledge the review, then press Play notes. Sound starts only after your tap. These are locally synthesized approximations, not recordings of real instruments.

    What plays?

    Detected pitches, accidentals, rests, chords, multiple voices, written durations (including dots and tuplets), ties, simple repeats, tempo markings, dynamics, staccato, tenuto and accents. This depends on Audiveris recognizing the page correctly.

    What needs care?

    Grace notes, ornaments, pedal, fermatas, hairpins, ottava, playing techniques, alternate endings and D.C./D.S./coda navigation are not fully performed. Warnings describe detected unsupported symbols; an unrecognized symbol cannot always be flagged. No automatic perspective correction or manual score editor is included. Export MusicXML to correct the score in a notation editor.

    Your photo stays in your space.

    Camera preview and audio run in your browser. The photo is sent only after you press “Read the notes”, to this app’s server, not a third-party OMR API. Temporary photos and OMR files are deleted after processing (including failures). Results stay in server memory for up to 30 minutes and use an unguessable link. This app has no accounts: use a trusted private deployment for sensitive music. No analytics, external fonts or external audio downloads.

    Camera & sound

    Live camera needs HTTPS or localhost and your camera permission. If unavailable, use your phone’s native camera picker or Upload photo. Convert HEIC to JPEG if needed. If you cannot hear playback, raise device volume, check silent mode and tap Play again.
